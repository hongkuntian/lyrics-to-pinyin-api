#### 4.2.1 Row meaning and claim identity

Let `a=attempt_count`, and let `f` mean genuine failed claims already charged to this job. A fresh initial job is `pending,a=f=0,claim_epoch=0`, with NULL claim nonce/until and NULL completion/retention fields. In settled pending state, `a=f`. The outbox row plus its state, epoch and nonce identify the one outstanding debit. `claim_epoch` remains monotone across both successes and failures.

The complete captured envelope E includes environment, job ID/key/kind, exact closed payload including selector/cutoff/phase/cursor/repair lineage, all NULL reference fields, state, expected `attempt_count`, `claim_epoch`, nonce hash/until, original created/due/retry metadata, and observed completion/delete fields. Source/participation/room/generation predicates are additional authority checks, not substitutes for E. Use exact JSONB value equality and safe-uint comparisons, not mutable callback assertions. A new claim gets a fresh cryptographic nonce and exactly epoch+1; safe-uint exhaustion refuses with retained debt, never wraps or resets.
